# Favicon CWO 2026

Vlastní minimalistický znak činky z devíti teček: tři tyrkysové tečky vlevo, tři růžové vpravo a tři bílé uprostřed, na tmavé zaoblené dlaždici. Na [loňské logo](https://github.com/czechweightliftingopenadmin-dev/cwo_web_lepsi/blob/main/public/logo512.png) navazuje motiv činky a tyrkysová, na letošní plakát tečkovaná geometrie a paleta. Návrh vznikl vestavěným nástrojem imagegen.

Výstup je uložen v `public/favicon-2026.png` (256 × 256 px) a `public/favicon-2026.ico` (16, 32, 48, 64 a 256 px). Oba soubory odkazuje `index.html`. PNG i ICO zachovávají průhlednost mimo zaoblené rohy. Parametr verze v odkazech zajišťuje načtení nové varianty po úpravě.

## Zadání návrhu pro imagegen

Use the reference CWO logo ONLY as loose conceptual inspiration: weightlifting, a barbell, and its familiar cyan colour. Create a DISTINCT new ultra-minimal 2026 favicon, not a tracing or reconstruction. The new mark is JUST an abstract horizontal barbell made of EXACTLY NINE large filled circular dots arranged like a compact letter H: a vertical column of three cyan #72d4e8 dots on the left, a vertical column of three magenta-pink #d34b91 dots on the right, and three off-white #f5f3ef dots in a horizontal row connecting the middle left dot to the middle right dot. Nine dots total. The three center white dots must be evenly spaced between the two colored columns and aligned to their middle dots. All nine dots have equal diameter, large enough to read cleanly at 16 pixels, with clear small gaps like Bitcount Single poster typography. Symmetric simple geometry, generous but not excessive padding, the symbol occupies about 72 percent of the tile width. Flat deep black #101011 rounded square tile, transparent outside its rounded corners. This should feel like a bold dot-matrix tech sports brand, linked to last year's weightlifting through the barbell and cyan, and to the new website through round Bitcount dots, black, white, and pink. Do not show a person, athlete, head, arms, legs, outer circle, decorative ring, extra dots, letters, year, gradients, glow, shadow, shading, texture, noise or 3D. Deliver one centered square favicon graphic with absolutely flat solid colours.

## Finální úprava

Clean up this minimal nine-dot barbell favicon. Preserve exactly this nine-dot symbol, composition and colours: three large cyan dots in a left vertical column, three large pink dots in a right vertical column, three smaller off-white dots connecting them horizontally across the centre. Keep the symbol otherwise unchanged. Make the rounded square background absolutely uniform solid opaque #101011, including the whole lower-right area below the white dots. Remove the irregular transparent black hole / torn patch in that lower-right area completely. Remove ALL background texture, all gradients and all shading, including from every coloured dot: each dot must have a completely uniform solid flat fill. No lighting, no highlights, no photorealistic surface. Truly clean vector-like simple graphic. Only the four corner areas OUTSIDE the rounded square may be transparent; all pixels INSIDE the rounded square must be opaque. Deliver one square favicon image. No new details, no added dots, no ring, no lettering.
