# Favicon CWO 2026

Motiv navazuje na vzpěrače s činkou z [loga loňského webu](https://github.com/czechweightliftingopenadmin-dev/cwo_web_lepsi/blob/main/public/logo512.png). Letošní varianta je minimalistický symbol z 18 kulatých teček: tyrkysová postava a činka, růžové konce činky a tmavá zaoblená dlaždice. Tečky odpovídají stylu Bitcount z plakátu. Úprava vznikla vestavěným nástrojem imagegen.

Výstup je uložen v `public/favicon-2026.png` (256 × 256 px) a `public/favicon-2026.ico` (16, 32, 48, 64 a 256 px). Oba soubory odkazuje `index.html`. PNG i ICO zachovávají průhlednost mimo zaoblené rohy. Parametr verze v odkazech zajišťuje načtení nové varianty po úpravě.

## Zadání pro imagegen

Redesign this overly complicated dotted weightlifting favicon into an extremely minimal dot-matrix logo. Keep ONLY a very simple front-facing weightlifter holding a barbell overhead, made of about 18–22 large round dots total. Remove the outer dotted circular border entirely. Remove the sweeping decorative body curves, duplicate limbs, extra points, shading and purple accents. One slightly larger cyan dot for the head, two simple raised arms built from 2 dots each, a short vertical torso built from 2 dots, and two short splayed legs built from 2 dots each. A simple horizontal overhead barbell built from 5 cyan dots with a pair of large pink end dots. Use a clearly readable symmetric pictogram with large clean gaps between the round dots, not a detailed reproduction. The entire mark should read instantly at 16 pixels. Background: one solid deep black #101011 rounded square tile with generous padding; transparent outside rounded corners. Only two foreground flat colors: vivid cyan #72d4e8 and magenta pink #d34b91 for the barbell ends. No ring around the figure, no gradients, no texture, no shadow, no glow, no outlines, no letters, no numbers. One single centered square favicon, ultra-minimal tech/Bitcount circular dot style.
